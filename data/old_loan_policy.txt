LOAN APPROVAL POLICY (Version 1.0, 2022)

1. Scope
This policy applies to all personal loan applications at Sunrise Bank.

2. Eligibility
Applicants must be aged 21 to 60. The minimum credit score is 700.
Salaried applicants must have at least 2 years of continuous employment.
Self-employed applicants are not eligible for personal loans.

3. Approval Limits
A branch manager can approve loans up to 5 lakh.
Loans above 5 lakh must be approved by the regional credit committee.

4. Processing Time
Every application must be processed within 7 working days.

5. Documents Required
Identity proof, address proof, last 3 salary slips, and 6 months of bank statements.

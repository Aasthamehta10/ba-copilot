BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT: Digital Loan Approval Platform (Version 2.0, 2025)

1. Objective
Replace the manual loan approval process with an online platform to reduce turnaround time.

2. Eligibility Requirements
BR-01: The system shall accept applicants aged 21 to 65.
BR-02: The system shall require a minimum credit score of 650.
BR-03: Self-employed applicants shall be allowed to apply, with 2 years of business tax returns.

3. Approval Workflow
BR-04: The system shall auto-approve loans up to 3 lakh.
BR-05: Loans between 3 lakh and 10 lakh shall be routed to the branch manager.
BR-06: Loans above 10 lakh shall be routed to the regional credit committee.

4. Service Levels
BR-07: Every application shall be processed within 5 working days.

5. Documents
BR-08: Applicants shall upload identity proof, address proof, and 3 months of bank statements.
